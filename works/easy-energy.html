about.

This workshop, in collaboration with Tangity (part of NTT Data), explored the service design process through a real-world challenge: promoting the adoption of Home Energy Management Systems (HEMS) to support a greener energy transition in Italy.

key impact.

Among the four teams in the workshop, our solution was recognized as the most market-ready and viable for real-world implementation. Through thorough market research and user analysis, we ensured that Easy Energy aligns with current industry needs and consumer behavior, making it both feasible and impactful.

EasyEnergy service not only simplifies HEMS adoption but also bridges the gap between energy providers and homeowners, creating a scalable solution that could drive a greener and more efficient energy future in Italy.

Antonio Grillo, Emanuela Cozzi, Chaidi Gargareta, Jinlong Yu (Tangity Team)

situation.
goal.

Our goal was to design a service solution that addresses the needs of both:

  1. Electricity providers & grid operators — Encourage better energy distribution throughout the day and maximize the use of renewable energy, which depends on specific weather conditions.
  2. Homeowners — Provide a cost-effective and convenient way to use appliances while optimizing energy consumption.

How might we raise awareness about the potential benefits of utilizing smart home devices, address the privacy concerns of Italians and facilitate widespread adoption?
