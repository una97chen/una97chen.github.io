The Pavilion of Phoenix

about.

The Pavilion of Phoenix is located in the NTU Experimental Forest. The hotel team collaborated with the NTU Experimental Forest to craft a high-quality lodging experience. In this project, we helped the hotel with nature-based activities, creating a phygital (physical + digital) service solution through a service design approach.

key impact.

By applying a service design approach, this project not only encouraged guests to connect with nature through interactive gameplay, but also created a replicable business model.

The experience framework can be adapted to other resort locations, offering consistent, meaningful engagement that blends digital interaction with on-site exploration.

situation.
goal.

Our goal was to design a service solution that addresses the needs of both:

  1. Create a digital experience that enhances the natural adventure without overwhelming it.
  2. Design an experience that engages multiple age groups.

How might we use Chatbot to create an interesting immersive experience at the forest?

action.
results

Now, travelers can join the forest exploration game through their smartphones. Different age groups and routes offer unique experiences tailored to each user. This service transforms The Pavilion of Phoenix from simply a place to stay into a starting point for stepping into the forest and connecting with nature.
